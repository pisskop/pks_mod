***Penetrate Power****
-vermin can want to eat your food.  Some vermin can nibble through, dig into, or get around your barrels and pots into your food.  This is reflected in penetrate power.  In vanilla, rats have 2 penetrate power-
-as I understand it, the game rolls a random number for a container.  0-100 for metal, 0-95 for wood, 0-90 for cloth.  If the critter's penetrate power is greater than the number rolled, it gets in and eats at the food.-


[SELECT_CREATURE:RAT]
	[CV_CONVERT_TAG]
		[CVCT_MASTER:PENETRATEPOWER]
		[CVCT_TARGET:2]
		[CVCT_REPLACEMENT:3]
	[APPLY_CURRENT_CREATURE_VARIATION]

		-what IS probably more healthy is superstition, religious and historical variety, and snake-oil sales, all of which can inflate or deflate the value of any one thing or another-
